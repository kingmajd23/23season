# Marketing Plan: 110+ Live Viewers on 20/10

## The target, worked backward
| Step | Number |
|---|---|
| Live viewers needed | **110** |
| Show-up rate for free webinars | ~30–35% |
| Registrations needed | **~320 → target 350** |
| Days available | **9 (11/10 → 20/10)** |
| Registrations per day | **~40** |

**Main channel: WhatsApp.** For an Arabic-speaking audience, WhatsApp reminders are what make people actually show up.
Collect **phone numbers** on the sign-up form, not only emails.

---

## Where the 350 registrations come from

| Source | Target | How |
|---|---|---|
| Your own followers (Instagram / TikTok) | 120 | Reels + stories every day, link in bio |
| Paid ads (Meta: Instagram + Facebook) | 120 | ~1,500–2,500 ₪ budget at ~6–12 ₪ per registration |
| Current & past students + their friends | 50 | Referral push: "bring one friend who's always asking you about trading" |
| WhatsApp groups / broadcast lists | 40 | Personal messages, not spam |
| Collaborations (1–2 creators / pages in your niche) | 20 | Story mention or a joint live |

## Day-by-day plan

### Day 1–2 · Sun 11/10 – Mon 12/10 · SET UP
- [ ] Choose the platform: **Zoom webinar** (or Zoom meeting with a 300+ license). Test it.
- [ ] Build the registration page: name + **phone** + email. Use your landing page or a simple form.
      Copy: headline + subtitle from `01-headline.md`, 3 bullets ("what you'll see tonight"), date/time, photo of you.
- [ ] Make a **WhatsApp group or broadcast list** called "ويبينار 20/10" and send everyone who signs up into it automatically (thank-you page button: "انضم لمجموعة الواتساب عشان ما يفوتك الرابط").
- [ ] Prepare 3 ad creatives (see below) and launch ads by end of Monday.
- [ ] Post announcement: Reel + story + post.

### Day 3–5 · Tue 13/10 – Thu 15/10 · PUSH
- [ ] **1 Reel per day** (ideas below), each ending: "رابط التسجيل بالبايو، ويبينار مجاني 20/10"
- [ ] **5–8 stories per day**: countdown sticker, poll ("التريدينج معقّد؟ نعم / لا"), question box, sign-up screenshots ("وصلنا 150 مسجّل 🔥")
- [ ] Message current/past students with the referral text
- [ ] Reach out to 3–5 creators for a collaboration; aim to land 1–2
- [ ] Check ads every day: turn off the weak creative, put more on the best one

### Day 6–7 · Fri 16/10 – Sat 17/10 · SOCIAL PROOF
- [ ] Post a student result (with permission) as a Reel: "هاد [اسم] قبل 3 شهور ما كان بيعرف يفتح شارت…"
- [ ] Go **live on Instagram for 15 min**: answer trading questions, and keep saying "التفاصيل كلها بالويبينار، 20/10"
- [ ] Story: "وصلنا [عدد] مسجّل. المقاعد باللايف محدودة"

### Day 8–9 · Sun 18/10 – Mon 19/10 · LAST CALL
- [ ] Story every few hours: "باقي يومين / باقي يوم"
- [ ] Last Reel: "الغلطة اللي 90% بيعملوها… رح أفرجيكم إياها لايف بكرا"
- [ ] Raise ad budget by ~30% for the last 48 hours

### Day 10 · Tue 20/10 · WEBINAR DAY
- [ ] Send all reminders below (WhatsApp + email)
- [ ] Story every 2–3 hours, countdown sticker
- [ ] Go live **5 minutes early** so people joining early see you there
- [ ] Practice once with the slides and a timer in the morning

### Day 11–13 · Wed 21/10 – Fri 23/10 · FOLLOW-UP (where extra sales come from)
- [ ] Send the replay **only for 48 hours** to everyone who registered
- [ ] Message people who attended but didn't buy (texts below)
- [ ] Close sign-ups at the real deadline and announce it

## Reel ideas (one per day)
1. **The messy chart:** show your old chart with 15 indicators → cut to your clean chart. Text: "قبل 7 سنين vs اليوم"
2. **"The mistake":** "في غلطة 90% من المبتدئين بيعملوها… رح أحكيها بالويبينار 20/10" (open loop)
3. **"No time?":** "بتشتغل 9 ساعات؟ طالبي [اسم] كمان. بيحط 20 دقيقة باليوم"
4. **Myth-busting:** "3 أشياء حكولك عن التريدينج… وكلها غلط"
5. **Student result** (with permission)
6. **Behind the scenes:** you preparing the webinar. "عم بحضّرلكم إشي…"
7. **Countdown:** "بكرا. لايف. مجاني."

## Ad creatives (Meta)
- **A: Video of you talking (best performer usually), 20–30 sec:**
  "مفكّر التريدينج معقّد ومش إلك؟ أنا صرلي 7 سنين بالسوق، وبدي أفرجيك ليش هو أسهل بكتير مما بتفكر. ويبينار مجاني، لايف، 20/10. سجّل من الرابط."
- **B: Messy vs clean chart image** + headline "ما بتحتاج 20 إنديكيتور. بتحتاج إستراتيجية وحدة."
- **C: Student result** (with a small "النتايج مش مضمونة" line)

**Targeting:** Arabic speakers in your area, 20–45, interests: stock market, investing, trading, personal finance, entrepreneurship.
Also a **retargeting** audience: people who watched your videos or visited your profile in the last 30 days.

> ⚠️ Meta's ad rules are strict about income claims for financial services. Don't write promises like
> "make X a month" in ads, or the ads get rejected and the account can get restricted.
