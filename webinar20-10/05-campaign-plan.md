# Campaign Plan: "One Strategy" (Oct 12 → Nov cohort)

One campaign that promotes three things as a single funnel:

```
FREE GAME WEBSITE  ──►  FREE WEBINAR 20/10  ──►  90 STOX (4,900 ₪)
 (try it, get hooked)     (believe it's simple)     (learn it with me)
   awareness               consideration              decision
```

> **Assumption:** the free game website is a trading-related game (e.g. "guess the next candle"
> or a trading simulator). If it's something else, tell me and I'll adjust the game messaging.

## 1. Campaign Overview

- **Name:** *إستراتيجية وحدة* / "One Strategy"
- **Summary:** Show Arabic speakers that trading is simpler than they think: let them *play* it free, *see* it in a live webinar, then *learn* it in 90 Stox.
- **Primary objective:** **6–9 enrollments in 90 Stox** (≈ 29,000–44,000 ₪) by the cart close on **[23/10]**.
- **Secondary objectives:**
  - **350 webinar registrations → 110+ live viewers** on 20/10
  - **[500] game plays / [200] new contacts** (phone or email) collected from the game site by 20/10
  - Grow a WhatsApp list you own for future cohorts

## 2. Target Audience

**Primary: "the curious beginner"**
Arabic speaker in Israel, 20–45, employed or studying, has thought about trading/crypto but thinks it's complicated, risky, or needs too much time. Lives on Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp.

- **Pain points:** "too complicated", "no time", "afraid to lose money", "don't know where to start", "too many YouTube gurus"
- **Motivations:** extra income, financial independence, learning a real skill, being part of a community
- **Stage:** mostly *awareness → consideration*. The game catches them cold, the webinar warms them, the offer converts them.

**Secondary: "the frustrated self-learner"**
Already tried trading alone, lost money or got confused by indicators. Closer to buying, responds strongly to "you know too much, not too little."

**Tertiary: your current/past students**: referral engine and social proof source.

## 3. Key Messages

**Core message:** *التريدينج أسهل بكتير مما بتفكر. إستراتيجية وحدة بتكفي.*
(Trading is much simpler than you think. One strategy is enough.)

| Supporting message | Proof point |
|---|---|
| You don't need 20 indicators, you need one strategy | Messy vs clean chart; your 7 years |
| You don't need 8 hours a day | Student routine: under 30 min/day |
| Beginners can do it | Student who started from zero (with permission) |
| Knowing is 20%, doing it daily is 80%, that's why 90 Stox exists | Driving-lesson analogy; structured 90-day program |

**By product:**
- **Game:** "جرّب حظك… أو جرّب مهارتك 🎮 العبها مجاناً" → fun, zero risk, shows you can read a chart
- **Webinar:** "70 دقيقة لايف، مجاناً، وبتطلع بفكرة واضحة" → the open-loop hooks (the 90% mistake, last week's trade)
- **90 Stox:** "90 يوم. إستراتيجية وحدة. تتداول لحالك وبثقة." → results, structure, limited seats

> ⚠️ All channels: no income promises ("make X a month"). Show real results with
> "النتايج مش مضمونة". Meta rejects financial income claims and can restrict the ad account.

## 4. Channel Strategy

| Channel | Role | Format | Effort | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram + TikTok (organic)** | Reach + trust | 1 Reel/day, 5–8 stories/day, 1 IG live | High | 0 ₪ |
| **Meta ads (IG + FB)** | Main registration engine | 3 webinar creatives + 1 game creative + retargeting | Medium | ~2,200 ₪ |
| **WhatsApp** | Show-up rate + sales follow-up | Broadcast list, reminders, 1-on-1 follow-ups | Medium | 0 ₪ |
| **Free game website** | Lead magnet / list builder | Game → "want to learn the real thing? register free" CTA | Low (once built) | 0 ₪ |
| **Student referrals** | Warm, high-trust leads | Referral message + small thank-you | Low | ~300 ₪ (gifts) |
| **Creator collaborations** | New audiences | 1–2 story mentions or joint lives | Medium | ~500 ₪ (optional) |

**How the three connect on every channel:**
- Every **game** session ends with: "عجبتك؟ تعال للويبينار المجاني 20/10" → registration link
- Every **webinar** registrant gets the game link in the thank-you message (keeps them engaged until 20/10)
- The **webinar** sells **90 Stox**; the **game** stays live after as an always-on lead magnet for the next cohort

## 9. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **Registrations behind target** | Check daily; raise ads, add a Reel, extra student message batch |
| **Low show-up rate** | WhatsApp reminders (not just email), the "last week's trade" hook, bonus for staying to the end |
| **Meta rejects ads** (financial claims) | No income promises; disclaimer on results; keep the game ad separate and playful |
| **Game pulls attention away from the webinar** | Game always ends with a webinar CTA; game is the entry point, not the destination |
| **Webinar viewers don't buy on the night** | 48h replay + personal WhatsApp follow-ups + real deadline |
